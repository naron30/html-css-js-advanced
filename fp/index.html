UFC Legends

Khabib Nurmagomedov-The Eagle

Khabib Nurmagomedov – "The Eagle" Khabib Nurmagomedov is a retired Russian mixed martial artist and one of the greatest UFC fighters of all time. He competed in the lightweight division and finished his career undefeated with 29 wins and 0 losses.

Demetrious Johnson-Mighty Mouse

Demetrious Johnson – "Mighty Mouse" Demetrious Johnson is an American mixed martial artist and one of the greatest fighters in MMA history. He competed in the flyweight division and became UFC Flyweight Champion. He was famous for his speed, wrestling, technique and incredible fighting skills.

Max Holloway VS Conor Mcgregor


In 2026, Max Holloway and Conor McGregor faced each other again at UFC 329 on July 11 in Las Vegas, 13 years after their first fight in 2013. This time, Holloway won by first-round TKO after McGregor suffered a serious knee injury, ending the fight after just 69 seconds.

Tom Aspinall Vacates The Heavyweight Belt


On September 14, 2026, Tom Aspinall officially announced that he was vacating the UFC heavyweight championship because of ongoing complications with a serious eye injury.
